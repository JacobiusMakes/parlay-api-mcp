# Support

ParlayAPI is founder-run. Questions and bug reports land directly with the person who wrote the code, and replies are usually same-day. That is what usually happens, not a guaranteed SLA.

## Where to go

- **Bug reports:** [open an issue](https://github.com/JacobiusMakes/parlay-api-mcp/issues). Confirmed bugs usually get a fix the same day.
- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/JacobiusMakes/parlay-api-mcp/discussions)
- **Email:** support@parlay-api.com
- **Docs:** https://parlay-api.com/docs

## What helps

For bugs, the fastest turnaround comes from: your `parlayapi-mcp` version, which MCP client you are using (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, other), your client config snippet, and what the tool call returned versus what you expected.
